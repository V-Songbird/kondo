# Code of Conduct

## Our expectations

Everyone is welcome to participate regardless of age, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, religion, sexual
orientation, or level of experience. Treat people with respect, make room for
other perspectives, and give constructive feedback about the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks,
and sharing another person's private information without permission are
unacceptable. Respect requests to stop unwanted contact.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply in this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other spaces maintained for Kondo. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful
content, warn participants, or restrict participation in response to violations.
Responses should reflect the impact and severity of the behavior.

## Reporting

Do not post sensitive conduct reports or personal evidence in public issues.
Use GitHub's built-in reporting controls for abusive content or accounts.
A private project conduct contact has not yet been designated; the repository
owner must name that contact and an escalation route, including for concerns
about a maintainer. No project response time or confidentiality guarantee is
promised here. Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
